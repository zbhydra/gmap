Extension questions

Contact support
Is the extension free to use?

Yes. The extension works without an account and includes 1,000 records every month. Paid plans on the Pricing page raise the limits when you need more.

Which browsers are supported?

MapsGrab targets Microsoft Edge (Edge Add-ons) and Firefox (Firefox Add-ons). Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers load the extension directly from the release zip — see the install steps above.

Do I need to sign in?

No. Core extraction works without an account. Quota is counted per signed-in account, or per browser device when you are not signed in — sign in with the account that owns a plan so its higher ceiling applies.

What counts as a record?

A record is one business row collected from Google Maps. Reviews, photos and enrichment attached to that business do not count as extra records.

What happens when Google Maps changes?

Extraction rules ship as remote configuration, so fixes reach the panel without reinstalling the extension. If a page can't be read, the panel shows an error instead of failing silently.

Does MapsGrab see the data I collect?

Your settings and results are stored locally in your browser. Our servers only handle remote configuration, account and quota status, optional email/social enrichment, and minimal usage logs.

Where does the data come from, and is it legal to collect?

MapsGrab only collects information that is publicly displayed on the Google Maps pages you visit — the same details any visitor can read. We do not access private accounts, gated content, or non-public data.
