Privacy Policy — MapsGrab

Last updated: 2026-08-30

MapsGrab ("the extension") collects publicly visible business information from Google Maps pages that you browse and lets you export it. This policy explains what data the extension handles, where it is stored, and what network requests it makes.

1. Data the extension processes

  • Public business data — information displayed on the Google Maps pages you visit (name, address, phone, website, rating, reviews, photos, etc.) while you use the extraction features.
  • Your settings — preferences you configure (export format, field selection, automation switches, interface language).
  • Technical and interaction data — minimal usage events (feature usage counters, error messages) used to keep the extension working against Google page changes.
  • Account identifiers we issue — an anonymous device identifier and, if you sign in, your account email and plan/quota status.

The extension does not collect browsing history beyond Google Maps pages, and does not read the content of other websites you visit.

2. Where data is stored

  • Settings, exports-in-progress and batch task state are stored locally in your browser (extension local storage / IndexedDB). They never leave your device unless an export or integration below is triggered.
  • Google Drive / HubSpot OAuth tokens (if you connect them) are stored locally in your browser.

3. Network behavior

  • Our backend API — the extension contacts our servers for remote configuration (page-structure presets), account/quota services, business email/social-profile enrichment, and usage logging.
  • Google pages you browse — the extension reads the Google Maps pages you have open to extract the business data shown there.
  • Google Drive / HubSpot (optional) — only if you explicitly connect them in settings, exported data is uploaded to your own Drive or synced to your own HubSpot account.

The extension contains no ads, no third-party trackers, and no analytics networks. It does not sell or rent data to anyone.

4. Data removal

Removing the extension deletes all locally stored data. If you want your backend account data deleted, contact us and we will remove it.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@example.com

隐私政策 — MapsGrab

更新日期:2026-08-30

MapsGrab(下称「本插件」)处理你在 Google Maps 上浏览页面的公开商家信息,并支持将其导出。本政策说明本插件处理哪些数据、存储在哪里、发起哪些网络请求。

一、本插件处理的数据

  • 公开商家数据——你使用采集功能时,所浏览 Google Maps 页面上展示的信息(名称、地址、电话、网站、评分、评论、照片等)。
  • 你的设置——你配置的偏好(导出格式、字段勾选、自动化开关、界面语言)。
  • 技术与交互数据——最小化的使用事件(功能使用计数、错误消息),用于在 Google 页面改版时维持插件可用。
  • 我们签发的账号标识——匿名设备标识;若你登录,还包括账号邮箱与套餐/配额状态。

本插件不收集 Google Maps 之外的浏览历史,不读取你访问的其他网页内容。

二、数据存储位置

  • 设置、进行中的导出与批量任务状态均存储在你的浏览器本地(扩展本地存储 / IndexedDB),除非触发下述导出或集成,不会离开你的设备。
  • Google Drive / HubSpot 的 OAuth 令牌(若你连接)同样仅存于浏览器本地。

三、网络行为

  • 我们的后端 API——本插件会访问我们的服务器,用于:远程配置(页面结构预设)、账号/配额服务、商家官网 Email/社媒补全、使用情况记录。
  • 你浏览的 Google 页面——本插件读取你打开的 Google Maps 页面,以提取其中展示的商家数据。
  • Google Drive / HubSpot(可选)——仅当你在设置中显式连接后,导出数据才会上传到你自己的 Drive 或同步到你自己的 HubSpot 账号。

本插件不含广告、不含第三方追踪器、不含数据分析网络,不出售或出租任何数据。

四、数据删除

卸载本插件即删除所有本地存储数据。如需删除后端账号数据,请联系我们处理。

五、联系方式

对本政策的疑问:support@example.com
